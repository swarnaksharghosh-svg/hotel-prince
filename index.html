01 The Hotel

About Hotel Prince

Hotel Prince stands on NH117, opposite Kakdwip College, a resting place on the long road south. Pilgrims bound for Gangasagar, families heading for the beach at Bakkhali and people here on business all stop for the same things: a quiet room, a good meal and a warm welcome at any hour.

Behind a paved forecourt with free parking and an EV charging point, our air-conditioned rooms are kept the way travellers hope for and rarely find on a highway: spotless, quiet and made up fresh every day. Several open onto balconies.

Downstairs, the restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and a buffet dinner, and the kitchen keeps cooking for room service through the night. The team speaks Bengali, Hindi and English, and is happy to help you plan the next leg of your trip.

03 Dining

A kitchen guests write home about.

Ask guests what they remember about their stay, and the food usually comes first.

The restaurant is small and warm, with table service through the day and a buffet dinner every evening. North Indian favourites share the menu with Bengali home cooking: dal, rice, seasonal vegetables and fish from Kakdwip's own harbour. Vegetarian guests are very well looked after (the palak paneer has its admirers), and room service runs around the clock.
